# 🎮 Enhanced MA Driver's Ed Game - NEW FEATURES!

## ✨ What's New - THREE Major Features Added!

### 1. 🚗 **DRIVING SIMULATOR** (Brand New!)
Drive a car and make real-time decisions!

- **Visual car driving down a road** - Watch an animated car from a top-down view
- **Real scenarios appear** - Stop signs, intersections, lane markings, parking spots
- **Make quick decisions** - You have 15 seconds to answer each question
- **See the action** - The car responds when you make the right choice
- **Score points** - Build up your score and maintain streaks

**How to Play:**
1. Click the **"🎮 Drive Simulator"** button (has a "NEW!" badge)
2. Watch the car drive down the road
3. When a scenario appears, read the question
4. Pick the right answer within 15 seconds
5. Watch your score grow!

### 2. 📖 **LESSONS LEARNED** (Adaptive Learning!)
The game now remembers what you struggle with!

- **Tracks every question** you answer
- **Prioritizes your weak areas** - Questions you get wrong often appear at the top
- **Three priority levels:**
  - 🔴 **High Priority** - Questions you get wrong >70% of the time
  - 🟠 **Medium Priority** - Questions you get wrong 40-70% of the time
  - 🔵 **Low Priority** - Questions you're doing well on
- **Shows statistics** - See how many times you got each question right/wrong
- **Accessible anytime** - Review from the main menu or after completing a quiz

**How to Use:**
1. Click **"📖 View Lessons Learned"** button on the main menu
2. See all questions ranked by how much you struggle with them
3. Focus on the high-priority ones!
4. The more you play, the smarter the system gets

### 3. 🔊 **AUDIO & PRESSURE SYSTEM** (Makes it Real!)
Feel the pressure like the real test!

- **Sound effects:**
  - ✅ Pleasant tone when you get it RIGHT
  - ❌ Lower tone when you get it WRONG
  - 🔊/🔇 Toggle sound on/off (button in bottom-right corner)

- **15-second countdown timer on EVERY question:**
  - Visual countdown bar at the top
  - Shows exactly how much time you have left
  - Turns RED and pulses when you're running out of time (<5 seconds)
  - Adds pressure just like the real test!

- **Works in all modes:**
  - Practice Mode - Learn with time pressure
  - Test Mode - Simulate real exam conditions
  - Survival Mode - Beat the clock and stay alive
  - Simulator Mode - Make quick driving decisions
